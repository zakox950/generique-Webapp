# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients — startups, founders, SMEs, creative directors — who land on Spyfie while evaluating dev studios. They're on a laptop or phone, comparing agencies, deciding in 10–30 seconds whether to reach out. They want to feel confident and slightly envious.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio site for Spyfie, a digital studio that builds web applications. Its job: convert a visitor into an inbound message. Success = someone clicks "Start a project" and fills in the contact form.

## Brand Personality

Charged · decisive · nocturnal. The studio that ships at 3am and makes it look effortless.

## Anti-references

- Bland "We build websites" agency templates with stock photos of laptops
- Cream-and-sage "warm studio" vibes — wrong register, wrong mood
- SaaS landing pages with metric grids and blue gradients
- Previous iteration: Blue/navy palette with fake statistics and long paragraph text

## Design Principles

1. **Minimum effective dose.** Every word, every element earns its place. If removing it doesn't hurt, remove it.
2. **Orange or black.** The brand lives in the contrast between pure dark and the single orange charge. Nothing decorates what the color already does.
3. **Work speaks first.** The projects section is the real product. Everything else clears the path.
4. **Motion is intentional.** Animations reveal information; they don't decorate or signal "this was made."
5. **Short is loud.** Three-word taglines hit harder than three sentences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` cuts all entrance/ambient animations to instant or crossfade. Focus rings visible on all interactive elements.
